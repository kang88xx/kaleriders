## Accessibility
Keep semantic keyboard-operable tabs, service buttons that expose their steps with `aria-describedby`, visible focus, retained FAQ semantics, accessible image/character labels, native video controls and no video autoplay. Retain reduced-motion handling and gallery pause on hover/focus/manual interaction, offscreen and hidden tab. The dialog keeps a 44px close control visible outside its scrolling form, traps modal focus natively, and restores trigger focus. Character movement instructions remain available to assistive technology without cluttering the visual page.

## Responsive behavior
Validate 320, 360, 390, 768 and 1440 CSS pixels in Korean and English. No page overflow. Above 900px the five services sit side by side with their lines; at 900px and below they form a vertical list on a rail into the team card, and the label keeps the height of its longest state so choosing a service never moves the page. Service text stays at 12px or larger. Reports stack on small screens; mobile metric rows remain readable. Gallery retains 20×5 desktop / 10×10 compact squares in a fixed frame.

## Interaction states
Inquiries open without a preset service and preserve existing selections and user-entered fields. Form required-field and email validation, pending, confirmed-success and error states remain intact. Actual transmissions are not made during QA. Language switches preserve tab state, the highlighted service and media nodes. The header places EN before 국문. Plain entry and invalid language queries default to English, ignoring old localStorage choices; explicit `?lang=ko` and `?lang=en` are respected. Switching languages updates the URL so reloading the chosen language still works; navigating back to a bare URL restores English.

Expanded photos change randomly every 2 seconds when active. Other tiles remain square, push sideways and dim, while the selected image has the approved border. Team auto-previews one random member every 3 seconds, with a 350ms coin flip, a 1-second PFP hold, and a return; only one member is active. Manual controls and visibility/reduced-motion safeguards remain.
